# Harbour Mutual Bank - Annual Report 2025

> Synthetic document for software testing. Harbour Mutual Bank is fictional; all numbers are invented.

## About Harbour Mutual

Harbour Mutual Bank is a customer-owned authorised deposit-taking institution serving members
across regional New South Wales and Queensland. As a mutual, profits are reinvested for the
benefit of members rather than distributed to shareholders. Net profit after tax for the year
ended 30 June 2025 was $41.7 million.

## Capital adequacy

Harbour Mutual applies the simplified capital framework for smaller ADIs. At 30 June 2025 the
Common Equity Tier 1 ratio was 15.8 per cent (2024: 15.3 per cent), and the total capital ratio
was 17.6 per cent. Because Harbour Mutual has no Additional Tier 1 instruments on issue, the
Tier 1 capital ratio equals the CET1 ratio. Risk weighted assets totalled $3,120 million at
30 June 2025, up from $2,985 million. The Bank is not required to report a leverage ratio under
the simplified framework.

## Liquidity

As a Minimum Liquidity Holdings (MLH) ADI, Harbour Mutual is not subject to the Liquidity
Coverage Ratio or Net Stable Funding Ratio requirements. The MLH ratio was 16.4 per cent of
liabilities at 30 June 2025, against a regulatory minimum of 9 per cent and an internal target
of 13 per cent.

## Loan portfolio and credit quality

Gross loans and advances grew 6.1 per cent to $4,210 million, with owner-occupied housing
representing 78 per cent of the portfolio. Arrears remained low: loans 90 days or more past
due plus impaired loans represented 0.42 per cent of gross loans at 30 June 2025 (2024: 0.37 per
cent). The credit impairment charge for the year was $3.9 million, compared with a $1.2 million
release in the prior year, reflecting the adoption of a more conservative economic outlook in
the AASB 9 scenario weights.

Total expected credit loss provisions were $18.4 million, of which Stage 3 provisions on
credit-impaired loans were $4.1 million. The provision coverage ratio, defined as total
provisions to credit risk weighted assets, was 0.71 per cent.

## Market and interest rate risk

Harbour Mutual does not operate a trading book and therefore does not report a traded
market risk Value-at-Risk measure. Interest rate risk in the banking book is monitored through
economic value sensitivity, which was $6.2 million for a 200 basis point parallel shock.

The directors declare that the financial statements give a true and fair view of the financial
position of Harbour Mutual Bank as at 30 June 2025.
