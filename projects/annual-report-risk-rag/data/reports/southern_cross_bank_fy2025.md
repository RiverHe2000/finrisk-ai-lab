# Southern Cross Bank Limited - Annual Report FY2025

> This is a synthetic annual report created for software testing. Southern Cross Bank Limited
> does not exist, and every figure below is fictional.

## Chairman's letter

The Board is pleased to report a resilient year for Southern Cross Bank. Statutory net profit
after tax was $2,310 million, up 4 per cent on the prior year, and the Board declared a fully
franked final dividend of 92 cents per share. Our purpose remains to build a better financial
future for our customers, and the investments we made in technology and risk capability during
the year position the Group well for FY2026.

## Chief Executive's review

Operating income grew 3 per cent to $9,845 million, driven by home lending growth of 5 per cent
and disciplined deposit pricing. Operating expenses rose 2 per cent as we continued to invest in
digital channels and fraud prevention. The cost-to-income ratio improved to 43.1 per cent. We
remain focused on simplification and on maintaining strong capital and liquidity buffers.

## Capital management

The Group manages capital in accordance with APRA Prudential Standard APS 110. Our Level 2
Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio was 12.4% at 30 June 2025, compared with 12.1% at 30 June 2024,
and comfortably above the 10.25% regulatory minimum including the capital conservation buffer.
The Tier 1 capital ratio was 14.2% and the Total capital ratio was 18.9% at 30 June 2025.
Total risk-weighted assets increased by 3.2% to $215,640 million, reflecting growth in credit
risk-weighted assets partly offset by the roll-off of a legacy market risk position.
The APS 110 leverage ratio was 5.3% at 30 June 2025 (30 June 2024: 5.2%).

## Funding and liquidity

Liquidity is managed under APS 210. The Group's quarterly average Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)
was 134% for the June 2025 quarter, and the Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) was 121% at
30 June 2025, both well above the 100% regulatory minimum. Customer deposits funded 74 per cent
of total funding. During the year we issued $14.2 billion of term wholesale funding across senior
unsecured, covered bond and Tier 2 formats.

## Credit quality

Credit quality remained sound. Loan impairment expense was $612 million for the year, or
9 basis points of average gross loans, compared with $548 million in FY2024. The increase
reflected a modest deterioration in the unsecured consumer portfolio and higher collective
provisions for the commercial property sector. The non-performing loans ratio, comprising loans
90 or more days past due and impaired assets as a percentage of gross loans and acceptances,
rose to 0.94% at 30 June 2025 from 0.81% a year earlier.

Total provisions for expected credit losses were $5,480 million at 30 June 2025. Provisions
comprised Stage 1 provisions of $1,210 million, Stage 2 provisions of $2,790 million and
Stage 3 provisions of $1,480 million. The provision coverage ratio, being total provisions
as a percentage of credit risk-weighted assets, was 1.58% at 30 June 2025 (30 June 2024: 1.55%).

## Market risk

Traded market risk is measured using a Value-at-Risk (VaR) model at a 99% confidence level over
a one-day holding period. Average traded market risk VaR was $18.6 million for the year ended
30 June 2025 (FY2024: $16.9 million), with a maximum of $31.2 million. Non-traded interest rate
risk in the banking book is managed within Board-approved limits and is discussed in Note 31.

## Model risk management

The Group's model risk management framework, aligned to APRA CPG 223, requires independent
validation of all material models before use and at least every three years thereafter.
During FY2025, 47 models were validated, including the retail probability of default and
loss given default models used for both regulatory capital and AASB 9 expected credit loss
purposes. Findings are tracked to closure by the Model Risk Committee.
